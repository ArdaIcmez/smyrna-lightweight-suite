  precedence over shorthand pack comments. We do not claim ownership of those data.

DATA_MANIFEST.json records snapshot, source ZIP hash, input hashes, coverage and
pack sizes. No drop rates were invented or filled in for missing sources. All
published observed rows passed percentage/count consistency validation at build.
Names and Classic estimates can differ from current Forever behavior.
