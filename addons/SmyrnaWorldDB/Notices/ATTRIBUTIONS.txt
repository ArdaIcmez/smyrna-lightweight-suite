SmyrnaWorldDB - Upstream Data & Asset Attributions
==================================================

SmyrnaWorldDB is a compiled, TOC-backed reference package for WoW Forever (client 16001)
assembled from the following upstream community datasets and projects:

1. QuestieDB (Questie Project)
   - Scope: Classic & Forever quest metadata, NPC spawn coordinates, quest-item drop
     sources, world object coordinates, zone area-to-uiMapID tables, and quest XP tables.
   - License: GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

2. pfQuest (Shagu / pfQuest contributors)
   - Scope: Supplemental Classic entity/spawn reference data cross-referenced in QuestieDB.
   - License: MIT License.

3. ForeverDungeonJournal
   - Scope: Classic & WoW Forever dungeon/boss/rare listings, dungeon quest rewards,
     encounter IDs, entrance coordinates, and custom Forever interior dungeon map layouts.
   - Attribution preserved for all ForeverDungeonJournal contributors.

All compiled data is stored read-only in SmyrnaWorldDB.toc and loaded on demand by
SmyrnaSuite (WorldDB.lua).
